# FSI CRM design direction

Source of truth: `docs/design/reference-overview.jpg`, the "Overview" dashboard the project owner supplied on 2026-11-12. The owner asked for the CRM to match it exactly: same layout, type, controls and chart style. This file transcribes it. When this file and the image disagree, the image wins. Brand assets: `public/logo-fsi.jpg`.

The reference is a margin dashboard for another business. FSI's data is different (no revenue, targets or branches), so each reference slot is filled with real FSI data (see "Overview slot mapping"). Copy the look, never the content: no invented numbers, targets, money or labels.

Dial: ENERGY 2 / RHYTHM 2 / MOTION 1

## Identity
- Product: internal CRM for French Solar Industry (Bali). Users: admin, sales, digital marketing, secretary. Daily use on desktop, quick checks and chat takeover on phone.
- Personality: calm, numeric, honest. Numbers are the hero; chrome stays quiet; text is short.
- Identity motif: diagonal hatching inside chart bars (`.hatch`: the weekly bars and the pipeline waterfall). Solid fill marks "now" (current week, the final result); hatching marks history, totals and losses. Never decoration.
- Progress bars are always solid (owner's choice, 2026-11-13): any bar that fills a track (win rate, workload, column share, stage stepper, reply window) uses a solid color, never hatching.

Rule: one accent. Blue marks "now". Pink and green only carry meaning.

## Type
- Inter (variable), `tabular-nums` on every number. Reason: the reference is a neutral grotesk, and tabular figures keep KPI numbers aligned.
- Page title "Overview": 40px, weight 400, tracking -0.02em.
- KPI hero number: 72px desktop / 56px phone, weight 400, tracking -0.04em, line-height 1.
- Insight card number: 48px weight 400 white, with a 12px caption to its right.
- Card title: 17px weight 500. Card meta under the title: 12px muted. Row labels 13px; row values 15px weight 500.
- Sentence case everywhere. No uppercase tracked labels. Sidebar group label "Analyze": 12px muted.
- Text is short. One meta line per card. Explanations belong in a tooltip, the insight card or the callout, not as paragraphs inside every row.

## Shape and elevation
- Radius: cards 20px, controls and nav items 10px, pill controls and delta pills 999px, icon circles 999px.
- Cards are flat: 1px `line` border, no shadow. Only popovers, menus and dialogs carry a shadow, plus the one solid bar per chart (below).
- Bars, measured from the reference: hatch stripes are bold (2px stripe, 2.5px gap, 135deg) with nothing behind them (`.hatch`). Vertical history bars have 6px rounded tops and fade toward the baseline (`.hatch-fade`). The solid bar (current week, final result) is fully rounded with a soft glow in its own color (`.bar-solid`); it is the only glow, at most two per screen.
- Card padding 20px. Grid gap 12px. Page padding 24px (desktop), 16px (phone).

## Desktop layout (1280px and wider)
One screen, no page scroll for the core grid at 1440x900.

```
| sidebar 248px | Overview                       [date chip | vs ...] [scope] [export] |
|               | KPI card          | tall breakdown card | insight gradient card     |
|               | (row 1)           | (spans rows 1 and 2) | (row 1)                  |
|               | Last 12 weeks     |                     | Needs you                 |
|               | (row 2)           |                     | (row 2)                   |
```
- Three equal columns. Column 1 and column 3 each hold two cards; column 2 holds one card spanning both rows. Row 1 is about 55% of the height, row 2 about 45%.
- Below the grid, after a 24px gap, full width: a two-column row (Schedule | Active chats, one column under 1024px), then the Admin per-salesperson table and recently added.
- 768 to 1279px: two columns (KPI + weekly | breakdown), insight and needs-you below in two columns.
- Phone (< 768px): one column in this order: KPI, Needs you, Active chats, Schedule, insight, Last 12 weeks, breakdown, extras. Top bar with logo and menu button; the sidebar becomes a drawer.

## Sidebar (exactly as the reference)
1. Top row: logo mark (28px rounded square) + "FSI CRM" wordmark 19px weight 500, and on the right a 32px square ghost button that collapses the sidebar to an icon rail (state remembered per browser).
2. Search field: `surface-raised` pill-ish input (radius 10, 40px tall) with a search icon, placeholder "Search", and a `⌘K` key hint at the right. It opens a command palette that searches leads and customers by name, phone or business; Enter opens `/customers/<id>`. `⌘K` / `Ctrl+K` opens it from anywhere.
3. Primary nav: 40px rows, 18px outline icons, 14px labels. Active item: `ink` background, white text, radius 10.
4. "Analyze" label, then the secondary nav.
5. Bottom group: "Notifications" with a count badge (orange `sun` circle, white number) showing chats waiting for this person; it opens `/inbox`. Then "Settings" for Admin only (connections, see "Settings page"). Help is not shown until a page exists.
6. Divider, then the profile row: 36px avatar (photo or initials), name 14px weight 500, "Role · French Solar Industry" 12px muted (truncated), and a "…" button opening a menu with: Notifications on this device, Light or dark theme, Sign out.

## Page header controls (exactly as the reference)
- Right-aligned group, 40px tall pills, 8px apart.
- Date chip: a `canvas`-tinted pill containing a white `surface-raised` inner pill with a calendar icon, the range ("14 Oct to 12 Nov") and a chevron; after it, inside the outer pill, muted text "vs 14 Sep to 13 Oct" (only when the comparison period is real data; otherwise omit the "vs" text). Clicking opens a menu: Last 7 days, Last 30 days, Last 90 days.
- Scope pill (Admin only): icon + "Whole team" + chevron, opening a menu of salespeople.
- Export pill (download icon + "Export"): downloads a CSV of the leads behind the current view. Show it only when it works.
- These are buttons with menus, not native `<select>` boxes. Keyboard: Enter/Space opens, arrows move, Escape closes.

## Overview cards (look)
- **KPI card:** title + meta; a 36px circular "…" button top right (menu with real actions, or omit it). Hero number. A delta row: small white pill with a pink ▼ or green ▲ and the change, then muted "vs previous 30 days". One short sentence (13px). A solid blue progress bar (12px tall, rounded) with a small scale label under its left end; a black target tick and "Target N" label only when a real target exists. Then 2 to 3 divider-separated rows: label left, value right, optional delta pill.
- **Breakdown card (tall, middle):** title + meta, and a text link with chevron top right. A horizontal waterfall: each row is a label (left) and a value (right) on one line, with its bar on the line below. The first bar is a full-width grey hatched total; each following bar is a pink hatched segment offset to where the previous one ended, joined by thin dashed vertical connectors; the last bar is a short solid blue result. An x-axis scale under the chart (5 ticks, 11px muted). At the bottom, a `surface-raised` inner callout (radius 12) with one bold sentence and one muted line, both computed from the data.
- **Insight card:** the gradient (navy top-left to `sun` bottom-right) with a soft light arc shape in the lower right; a translucent outline chip with an icon at top left and "1 of 3" at top right (pager arrows on hover/focus or below on phone); big number + caption; 19px white headline; muted white body; a white pill button with chevron at the bottom. One per screen.
- **Needs you:** title + meta ("4 things today"), "…" button. Rows: 32px tinted icon circle (pink, amber, blue, grey), title 13px weight 500, one detail line 12px muted, chevron. Dividers between rows. Each row opens the matching filtered list.
- **Last 12 weeks:** title left, muted meta right. Thin bars (about 60% of the slot width) with gaps, hatched blue; the current week solid blue with its value above it. Dates only under the first and last bar. A dashed target line with its label only when a real target exists. No per-bar numbers, no legend, no paragraph.

## Overview by role
The reference Overview is the Admin's view. The other roles get a shorter page with only their own work. Every query runs as the signed-in user, so RLS limits it to their leads and chats.
- **Admin:** the top grid, then Schedule | Active chats, then By salesperson and Recently added. Header: date chip, scope pill, Export.
- **Sales, Digital Marketing, Secretary:** title "Overview" with no header controls, then Needs you (full width), then Schedule | Active chats. No win rate, waterfall, insight, weekly chart, team table, date chip or Export, and no team-wide number anywhere.
- The kanban board is not on the Overview for any role (owner's decision, 13 Nov 2026). It lives at `/pipeline`.
- No card or chart sizes itself from the window height: on a tall screen nothing stretches.

## Schedule card
- Title "Schedule", meta "Today and the next 14 days". Source: the `appointment`, `site_survey` and `follow_up_date` columns on leads, read as Bali days. `quotation` and `closing` are past milestones in the legacy app, not scheduled dates, so they are left out.
- Left (top on a phone): a mini calendar of the whole weeks that hold the window, Monday first, 32px cells, 12px tabular numbers. Today is an `ink` circle; days outside the window are `muted`; a 4px `accent` dot marks a day with items. It is hidden from screen readers because the list says the same.
- Right: the list, grouped by day ("Today, Fri 13 Nov", "Tomorrow, ...", then the date), 12px muted group headings. Rows as in Needs you: 32px grey icon circle (calendar for an appointment, map pin for a site survey, calendar clock for a follow-up), name 13px weight 500 linking to `/customers/<id>`, one 12px muted line with the type and, for Admin, the salesperson. The list scrolls inside itself past 420px.
- Overdue follow-ups on open leads come first under a `negative` heading with the count, pink icon circles and "Was due <date>"; at most five, then a link to the full list. A follow-up on a lead at Deal or No deal is not shown.
- Empty: "Nothing is scheduled" and how to add a date. Last line, 12px muted: "Google Calendar is not connected yet." while the Google keys are missing. Nothing else may suggest a calendar connection until one exists.
- Booked schedules (`lead_schedules`) join the list under their Bali (WITA) office day: their row line reads "10:00 WITA · Call", or for another zone "10:00 WIB (11:00 WITA) · Call" (the whole line is already muted, so the Bali time goes in brackets), or "All day", with the kind's icon (phone for a call). A header link "Calendar" opens `/calendar`.

## Schedules and calendar
- **Kinds and dots:** Appointment (calendar, `accent` dot), Site survey (map pin, `--st-first-meeting` teal), Call (phone, `--st-first-contact` indigo), Follow-up (calendar clock, `warning` amber); anything overdue is a `negative` dot. Existing tokens only. The kind is always named in text, so a dot is never the only signal.
- **Time zones (owner request 2026-11-13):** FSI's customers are across Indonesia, so each booking keeps the customer's zone: WIB (UTC+7), WITA (UTC+8, Bali, the office) or WIT (UTC+9). Every time on screen carries its label. Staff views show the customer's local time first, "Tue, 17 Nov · 10:00 to 11:30 WIB", then, only when the zone is not WITA, the Bali office time in `muted`: " · 11:00 to 12:30 WITA" (screen readers hear "in Bali" after it; the Bali day is named too when it is another one, "Thu, 19 Nov, 00:30 WITA"). In a sentence (result lines, dialog descriptions) the Bali time goes in brackets: "(11:00 to 12:30 WITA in Bali)". A follow-up reads "· 09:30 WIB", all day "· All day" with no label. Each view says once "Times show the customer's local time with its zone; Bali office time in grey." (card footer, Calendar footer; the Calendar header meta is the short form).
- **Grouping:** the month grids, the agenda, the profile Schedule card and the Overview Schedule card group and order by the Bali (WITA) office day (`officeDay`), soonest first across zones, all-day items leading a day; an all-day item stays on its own day. Overdue uses the booking's own zone (an all-day item ends at midnight where the customer is).
- **Compact month** (profile card, phone calendar): Monday first, 44px day buttons (40px from md), today outlined with a 1px `ink` ring, the picked day filled `ink`, days outside the month `muted`, up to four 6px dots. Arrow keys move by day and week, Home and End to the week's ends, Enter picks; one day is in the Tab order. Picking a day narrows the list; picking it again shows everything.
- **Schedule card (customer profile):** left column under Next step. Header "Schedule", meta "N upcoming, N overdue", tinted "Book" pill. Month (272px) beside the list when the card is wide enough, above it otherwise. List: Overdue (pink heading telling them to mark done or cancel), Upcoming, and "Show past (N)" collapsed. A row: 32px icon circle (blue scheduled, pink overdue, grey past), title, kind and when, location and (Admin) salesperson, status pill (Scheduled, Overdue, Done, Cancelled; ink text on its tint), then two 24px badges: Google (synced, not connected, pending, failed) and email ("Email sent to x", not sent, not set up, failed). A failed badge is a button that shows the reason; "not connected" links Admins to `/settings`. "…" menu: Reschedule…, Mark done…, Cancel… Footer, 12px muted: how times are written, then what is not connected yet, with "Open Settings" for Admins.
- **Next step** shows the soonest upcoming schedule with its zone label and, outside WITA, the Bali time in `muted` (blue tint when its Bali day is today, plain later, with "See the schedule"), and falls back to the follow-up date as before.
- **Time zone picker (booking dialog):** "Time zone" segmented control after Type (first in a reschedule): one tinted `line/70` track, three 44px segments (40px from md) west to east, WIB, WITA, WIT, each with its UTC offset in 12px regular weight under it on a phone and beside it from md; the chosen one `ink` with `ink-contrast` text. Hover shows "WIB (Western Indonesia Time, UTC+7)"; that full name is the radio's name for screen readers. A new booking preselects `zoneForLocation(customer.location)`; a reschedule keeps the booking's zone. When the location is unknown, the picker sits on WITA with a `warning-soft` note (ink text): "Location unknown or unclear, so this is set to WITA. Check the customer's city."; when it names places in more than one zone, "The location names places in more than one zone; matched Jakarta, please check." The note goes away once the user picks a zone. Date, start and end are typed in the chosen zone, the date's minimum is today there, and outside WITA a 13px muted line under the times repeats them in Bali: "10:00 to 11:00 WIB · 11:00 to 12:00 WITA in Bali" (", the next day" or ", the day before" when Bali is on another day). Until the time zone database update is applied, a WIB or WIT save says "Nothing was saved: bookings in WIB are not switched on yet (a database update is still pending). Ask the owner to apply it, or book in WITA for now."
- **Booking dialog:** "Book" on the card, "Book a schedule…" in the profile "…" menu (on a phone that menu sits in the bottom bar). Type as radio pills, date (today or later), all day, start and end (end hidden for a follow-up, both for all day; empty end is one hour), title prefilled "<Type>: <name>" and following the type until edited, location prefilled from the lead, notes (staff only, never emailed), salesperson pill (Admin), and "Email the lead" with the address it goes to, or disabled with the reason. Reschedule reuses it with time zone, date and times only. After saving, the dialog shows one line per step (saved, old CRM date, Google, email): plain when done, muted when nothing to do, amber when not connected or no address, pink when it failed. A stale save says the item changed and offers Reload.
- **Cancel and Mark done** ask first. Cancel says whether the lead will be emailed a cancellation (only a lead who got the invitation, and only when email is set up).
- **Calendar page (`/calendar`):** nav "Calendar" after Pipeline. Header pager (previous, "November 2026", next, "This month"), then Month / Agenda toggle (1024px and up) and the Salesperson (Admin) and Type pills, all in the URL. Month view: seven flat columns, each day listing three items (dot, the customer's start with its label such as "10:00 WIB", customer; the Bali time in the tooltip and for screen readers) linking to the customer, then "N more" to the agenda on that day; overdue pink, done struck through. Agenda and every phone: the compact month above (beside, when wide) the month's items grouped by day, each naming the customer. Moving, finishing and cancelling live on the customer's page.
- **Booking from the Calendar (owner request 2026-11-13):** an ink "New schedule" pill after the pager, and from today on every month-board day is a target: a click on its empty space, or its "+" (24px, shown on hover, keyboard focus and touch screens), opens the booking dialog on that day; on a phone and in the agenda, a picked day offers "Book on this day". The dialog starts with a "Customer" combobox (name, phone or business, the leads search, so Sales only find their own): each result shows name, business and location, and the status pill; arrows move, Enter or a tap picks. Then the form continues as on the customer page, with the customer and "Change" on top. After saving: the result lines plus "Open customer"; the calendar reloads behind it, and Close moves to the item's month when it is another one. On a phone the customer step fills the sheet.
- **Booked by AI:** what the WhatsApp assistant booked carries a neutral "Booked by AI" chip, plus an amber "To confirm" chip (ink text) until staff confirm it, on the profile card, the agenda and (as "AI" and "Confirm", full words for screen readers) the month board.
- **Not set up:** while the `lead_schedules` table is missing, the card and page say "Schedules are not switched on yet. The database update for schedules has not been applied." and nothing offers to book.

## Active chats card
- Title "Active chats", meta "Latest WhatsApp conversations", header link "Inbox". At most five rows from the inbox's own loader, chats that need a person first.
- Row: name or number 13px weight 500 with the relative time right ("5 min ago", "3 h ago", "2 d ago", then the date); one truncated 12px preview line; then who is answering ("Answered by AI", "Answered by <name>", "Answered by You", or "Staff" when the name cannot be read). Rows link to `/inbox/<id>`.
- A chat that needs a person (the inbox's `attentionOf` rule) gets the `warning-soft` row tint and a chip with the inbox's own words ("Waiting for staff", "Waiting for your reply").
- States: tables not set up, no chats ("No WhatsApp chats yet. They appear here once the number is connected."), error with "Try again".

## Pipeline page (`/pipeline`)
- **Filter bar** under the title, in the header pill style, URL-driven (`q`, `sales`, `type`, `mover`, `added`, `stuck`, `due`): a pill-shaped search box (name, phone or business), then menu pills for Salesperson (Admin only), Lead type, Moved last by (Anyone, AI, Person) and Added (any time, last 7, 30, 90 days), then two toggle pills, "Stuck over 14 days" and "Follow-up due", which fill with `ink` when on and use the same rules as the Needs you rows. Active filters repeat below as removable chips (white pill, 13px, an x) with "Clear all". On a phone the search stays and everything else folds into one "Filters" pill with the active count, opening a bottom sheet.
- **Board:** one column per status in pipeline order; leads with no status are not on it, and a quiet 13px muted link under the board gives their real count and opens them in Leads. Each column is a flat card (20px radius, `surface`, 1px `line`), 280px wide and 640px tall, scrolling inside itself; the board scrolls sideways inside its own container, never the page. On a phone a column is 85vw wide with scroll snap.
- **Column header:** short status label 14px weight 500, the count right (12px muted, always from a count query), and a 6px share bar scaled to the largest column: a solid share bar in the column's status color.
- **Status colors (owner request 2026-11-13):** each status has one soft color (`--st-*` tokens: New blue, First contact indigo, First meeting teal, Quotation amber, Second appointment violet, Deal green, No deal rose). The column gets a very light tint and a status dot, each card a soft tint and a matching border (`.tone-lane`, `.tone-card`, `.tone-dot`). The status name is always shown in text too, so color is never the only signal.
- **Lead card:** `surface-raised`, 12px radius, 1px `line`, 12px padding. Name 14px weight 500 (two lines at most) linking to the customer; business and location 12px muted; "N days in status" or "Status date unknown"; the follow-up date, as a pink chip with the word "overdue" when it has passed; then the salesperson (Admin), a neutral chip "AI" or "Person" for who set the status last (nothing when unknown), and the "Move to" button.
- **Moving:** drag a card to another column with a pointer (the target column shows a 2px `accent` outline), or use "Move to" on the card, which is the keyboard and touch path. Deal and No deal ask first in the shared dialog. The card moves at once at 60% opacity until the server answers; a refusal puts it back and shows the reason in a pink notice. After a move a notice offers "Undo" for 8 seconds, with a thin line counting down; the countdown waits while the notice is hovered or focused. This line is the one timed motion in the app.
- **Volume:** 20 cards per column, longest in the status first, then "Show more (N left)".
- **Empty:** a column with nothing says so in 12px muted; when no lead matches the filters the board is replaced by one empty state with "Clear all filters".

## Leads page (`/leads`)

- **Filter bar:** the shared pill filter bar (`ui/filter-bar.tsx`, also used by Pipeline): search box, then menu pills for Status, Salesperson (Admin only), Lead type and Added (any time, last 7, 30, 90 days, or "Pick dates..." in a dialog), and the sort pill at the right. - **Cards (below 1280px):** one column, two from 1024px. Name and status pill, then Partner tag, business and location, salesperson (Admin) and next step, then 44px Call and WhatsApp buttons and the "..." menu.
- **Pagination:** "1 to 100 of 3,010" with Previous and Next pills (44px on phones), 100 per page.
- **States:** the route's loading frame mirrors the layout; each quick view has its own empty message with the next action; filtered to nothing shows "Clear all"; a failed load shows the route error with "Try again".

## Motion
MOTION 1: hover and focus states only, 120ms color/background transitions. No entrance animations, no loops. One exception with a job: the 8 second countdown line on the pipeline board's Undo notice.

## Theme
Light by default for everyone, as in the reference, regardless of the device's dark setting. Dark is available from the profile menu and remembered per browser; it uses the same tokens and must work fully.

## Customers pages (`/customers`, `/customers/<id>`)
Leads is the work queue (what to do next); Customers is the contact book (who the people are). The two must not look like the same table.
- **List header:** "Customers" with the count muted under it ("3,010 customers", or "40 customers match" when filtered) and a "Work queue in Leads" pill on the right.
- **Filter bar:** a pill search box that applies as you type (300 ms after the last key, URL `q`, history replaced so Back does not step through letters; Enter applies at once; it also matches a number typed as "+62 812 ..." against the WhatsApp form of the phone). Then menu pills for Salesperson (Admin only) and Lead type, toggle pills "Has documents" and "Has WhatsApp chat" (ink fill when on), "Clear all" when anything narrows the list, and the order pill on the right (Newest added by default, Oldest added, Name A to Z). On a phone the search is full width and the pills wrap under it.
- **Rows (md and up):** one flat card around the list. Each row: 36px initials circle, name 14px weight 500 with a Partner tag, business and location 13px muted; the phone written for reading ("+62 812 3456 7890") with 36px round WhatsApp and Call buttons; the salesperson (Admin, initials and name); the status pill; a document icon with the count when there are any. The whole row opens the profile; the contact buttons keep their own action.
- **Cards (phone):** name, status pill, business and location, then 44px Call and WhatsApp buttons side by side, or "No phone number saved".
- **Pagination:** "101 to 200 of 3,010, page 2 of 31" with Previous and Next pills, 100 per page.
- **Profile header:** "< Customers" back link, the name at 40px, one line under it with the status pill, business, location and the salesperson (initials and name). Right: Call (ink pill), WhatsApp, Edit and a "..." menu (Change status..., Reassign salesperson... for Admin, Delete customer...). On a phone these become a bar pinned to the bottom edge with Call, WhatsApp and "..." (Edit moves into the menu); the page leaves room for it.

## Settings page (`/settings`, Admin only)
- **Job:** show the Admin which outside connections work and test each one without ever showing a key. Non-admins are sent to `/` on the server; every test action re-checks Admin. Sidebar: "Settings" (gear icon) under Notifications in the bottom group, Admin only.
- **Layout:** "Settings" title with one muted line ("Admin only. ..."), then service cards in two columns from 1024px (one column below), then a full-width "How to set these" card (Vercel, Project Settings, Environment Variables, redeploy, never paste keys into the CRM).
- **Card:** 32px icon circle (blue when connected, pink on error, grey otherwise), title 17px weight 500, one 13px muted purpose line, status pill at the right: Connected (green tint), Keys set, not tested (white with line), Not set up (sunken grey), Error (pink tint), Not built yet (dashed line). Then "Details" rows: only non-secret identifiers (Supabase project ref, last four digits of the WhatsApp phone number id, Graph version, OpenAI model) and last activity read from the database (last message received, last webhook problem, last AI reply, last AI problem, registered devices). Tests are read-only and time out after 8 seconds. Website lead capture has no button: it is shown as "Not built yet" with one paragraph on what it will do.

## Sales team page (`/sales`, Admin only)
- **Job:** show the Admin how each person is doing and where work piles up, then manage their login. Non-admins are sent to `/`. Every lead number is a `countLeads` count with the Overview's own rules (open = has a status and is not Deal or No deal; deals = moved to Deal in the period; follow-ups due and stuck over 14 days as in Needs you), so it matches the Overview's By salesperson table and the Leads tabs, and each one links to that list.
- **Header:** "Sales team" with "N people, N active"; right: the period pill (Last 7, 30 or 90 days, URL `period`) and the ink "Add salesperson" pill.
- **Summary strip:** compact cards in the KPI style (icon circle and 13px title, 44px number, one 12px muted line), each one a link: Open leads (team-wide, unassigned included), Deals (period), Follow-ups due, Chats waiting (hidden when the WhatsApp tables are missing). Four columns from 1280px, two below.
- **People:** "People" card with the Role and Sort pills (Most open leads, Name, Most overdue; URL `role`, `sort`). From 1280px one row per person under plain headers (Person, Open leads, Deals, New leads, Follow-ups due, Stuck, Chats); below that one card per person (two columns from 1024px). Name links to `/sales/<id>`; role is an outline pill. Workload: "N open" and "of N assigned", over a 6px bar scaled to the largest number of assigned leads on the team: a light grey fill is everything assigned, solid blue from the left is the open share, so a very large load reads as the outlier. Follow-ups due, stuck and chats waiting are pink when above zero; zero is muted and not a link. Inactive people come last under "Inactive, cannot sign in (N)", with a pink "Inactive" pill, muted name and numbers and a grey photo.
- **Person page (`/sales/<id>`):** "< Sales team", then a 56px photo or initials, the name as the 40px title, role pill, Inactive pill, "Signs in as ...", and Edit and Activate/Deactivate pills (no status action on your own account). A row with the period pill, "Their leads" (`/leads?sales=<id>`) and "Their board" (`/pipeline?sales=<id>`). Then four cards (Open leads of N assigned, Deals, New leads, Follow-ups due), the Overview's waterfall and Needs you for that person, their Active chats (taken over by or assigned to them, waiting ones first with the warning tint), Schedule filtered to them, and Recently added. A person with no leads gets one empty state instead of the pipeline cards.
- **Dialogs:** pill buttons (Cancel tinted, the action ink). Add: Name, Username (how they sign in, cannot be changed later), Starting password (at least 6 characters, not shown again); the account starts as Sales. Edit: Name, Role and Status as pill menus; on your own account Role and Status are shown locked. Deactivate asks first and says the person cannot sign in and their leads stay assigned until reassigned. Success shows as a green line after the dialog closes; a failure stays in the dialog as a pink notice and is never shown as saved. There is no delete and no password reset.
- **Change photo:** Admin only, for anyone including yourself: the "Change photo" pill on the person page, and on `/sales` each photo is the button (a small camera badge marks it). The dialog shows the current photo or initials at 96px, a file field (JPEG, PNG or WebP, 2 MB at most, checked before upload), a live preview, and Cancel / Save photo; with a photo, "Remove photo" asks first. The file goes to `sales-avatar/<id>/<timestamp>.<ext>`, then `avatar_url` is saved; a failed save takes the upload back and says so, and only files in the person's own folder are ever deleted.
- **States:** route loading frame, a route error with Try again (no partial numbers), "Only you are on the team so far", and "Nobody has the <role> role" with Show all roles.
